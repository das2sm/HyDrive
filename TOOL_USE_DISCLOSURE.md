# Tool-Use Statement

Interactive generative-AI coding and writing assistants supported
experiment-infrastructure design and implementation, code review and
debugging, analysis verification, visualization code, manuscript organization,
and language editing. They were not components of the evaluated driving policy
and did not generate or alter experimental observations. The campaign protocol
and estimands were specified before analysis of the final outcomes. The authors
executed the experiments and verified the code, outputs, statistics, citations,
figures, and prose, and accept responsibility for the paper and artifact.
